(partial "kodilabel" (dict "po" $.po "label" (replaceRE `.* (\d+)\]` "$1" .))) -}} {{- end -}} {{- $l = $l | replaceRE `\[CR\]` " " | replaceRE `\[/?(B|I|COLOR|LIGHT|UPPERCASE|LOWERCASE|CAPITALIZE)\b[^\]]*\]` "" | strings.TrimSpace -}} {{- return $l -}}
