These are the settings you see in Kodi, in _Add-ons → Services → LT2http → Configure_.

- **Setting** is the name shown in Kodi (English).
- **ID** is the key in Kodi's `userdata/addon_data/service.lt2http/settings.xml`, and the name of the matching [lt2http option]({{% relref "command-line.md" %}}).
- **Default** and **Values** show the default and the allowed values (for sliders, the range).
- Rows in _italics_ are hints Kodi shows inside the settings dialog.
