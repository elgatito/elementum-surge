**Elementum Burst** (`script.elementum.burst`) searches torrent providers (trackers) for Elementum.
When you play a movie or an episode, Elementum asks Burst, and Burst queries the enabled providers and returns the results.

- Install it from the ElementumOrg repository, see [Installation]({{% relref "/getting-started/installation.md" %}}).
- Choose providers in _Add-ons → Program add-ons → Elementum Burst → Configure_, on the _Public trackers_ and _Private trackers_ tabs.
  All settings are listed in [Configuration → Burst]({{% relref "/configuration/burst.md" %}}).
- Private trackers need a login, or [cookie sync]({{% relref "cookie-sync.md" %}}) for sites with captchas.

Burst is a fork of Quasar Burst.
