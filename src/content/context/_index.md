**Elementum Context** (`context.elementum`) adds an _Elementum_ entry to the context menu of movies and TV shows in Kodi.

Open the context menu on any movie or episode, choose _Elementum Menu..._ and then _Play_: Elementum searches and plays it.
It doesn't matter which scraper or add-on the item comes from, or whether it's a local or a virtual item:
if Kodi treats it as a movie or an episode, you can play it.

Install it from the ElementumOrg repository, see [Installation]({{% relref "/getting-started/installation.md" %}}).
